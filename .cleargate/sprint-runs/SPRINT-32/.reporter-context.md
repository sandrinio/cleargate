### STORY-033-02 — Parallel-Spawn Ledger Attribution (RUN_ID barrier-writer)

**How this advances the sprint goal:** This is the hard prerequisite for parallel waves. Per-story token attribution is *already silently broken* under any sub-agent fan-out (live-confirmed in the STORY-033-01 spike: workflow agents fire `SubagentStop` against the orchestrator transcript with `delta=0/0`, mis-tagging every story's cost onto the prior ledger row). Making the three on-disk surfaces RUN_ID-aware is what lets STORY-033-04's barrier write one correct row per `(storyId, agent_type)` under concurrency. Without this, the parallel wave the rest of the sprint enables would report garbage cost data.

**Cross-story coupling:** None within M1 (disjoint from 032-01). DOWNSTREAM: this story *defines the contract* that STORY-033-04 (`launch_wave.mjs`, the barrier, the schema-typed `verdict.tokens`) consumes in M2. 033-02 builds NONE of the launcher — it makes the marker, sentinel, and `.session-totals.json` RUN_ID-aware and makes `token-ledger.sh` no-op on an already-written barrier row. The barrier itself, the `verdict` discriminated union, and the `RUN_ID` minting are STORY-033-04's surface (explicitly out of scope here per story §1.3).
