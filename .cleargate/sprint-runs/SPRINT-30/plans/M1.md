4. `cleargate-cli/test/commands/init.node.test.ts` — append test asserting `assert.doesNotMatch(combined, /DEP0190/)` and `assert.doesNotMatch(combined, /DeprecationWarning/)`.

**Acceptance trace.** Two Gherkin Scenarios in story §2.1:
